Pho

It is the perfect combination of the rich, creamy texture of milk flan and the slight bitterness and distinctive, bold aroma of Vietnamese coffee.

Ingredients

  • Chicken eggs: 5
  • White granulated sugar: 100 grams
  • Fresh milk: 500ml
  • Lime: ½ fruit
  • Vanilla: 2 vials
  • Boiled water, cooled: 100ml

Steps

  1. Making Caramel

    Place a pot on the stove, add 100ml of cooled boiled water and 50g of white granulated sugar, and cook over low heat. Stir continuously until the sugar dissolves completely and turns a rich amber-brown color. Then, add the lemon juice, stir well, and turn off the heat.

    When making the caramel, if the mixture becomes too thick, you can add a little cooled boiled water to achieve the right consistency. Quickly pour a small amount of caramel into each mold and gently swirl it around to coat the bottom with a thin layer.

  2. Making the Egg and Milk Mixture

    Separate five egg yolks and place them in a bowl. Using a whisk, gently stir in one direction until the yolks are completely smooth. Be careful not to whisk too vigorously, as this creates air bubbles that can cause the cake to develop a pitted texture.

    Combine 50g of white granulated sugar and 500ml of fresh milk in a saucepan, then heat the mixture over medium heat until the milk is warm (do not let it boil). Pour the warm milk into the bowl of beaten eggs and gently stir until the ingredients are thoroughly blended into a smooth, uniform mixture.

    To ensure the flan is smooth and free of lumps, strain the mixture through a sieve several times until completely smooth, then stir in the vanilla to enhance the aroma. Pour the mixture into molds already coated with a layer of caramel and cover them; Be careful not to overfill—fill only about three-quarters of the way—to prevent the flan from overflowing during cooking.

  3. Steaming Flan

    Place the flan molds into the steamer and steam for 30–40 minutes. After the required time, test for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the flan; if it comes out clean with no liquid oozing out,the flan is fully cooked.

  4. Enjoy

    Once the steamed flan has cooled slightly, it is ready to eat. Alternatively, you can refrigerate it for about two hours, then turn it out onto a plate, add shaved ice, drizzle with a little coffee, and enjoy.
