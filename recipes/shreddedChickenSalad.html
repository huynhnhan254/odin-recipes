Shredded Chicken Salad

A famous traditional Vietnamese dish featuring a harmonious blend of sour, spicy, salty, and sweet flavors, along with a refreshing quality that makes it very easy to enjoy.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 whole chicken (approx. 2kg)
  • Rice: 300g
  • Fried shallots: half a bowl
  • Lime: 1
  • Garlic: 4 cloves
  • Hot chili pepers: 4
  • Yellow Onion: 300g
  • Button mushrooms: 200g
  • Scallions: 4 stalks
  • Shallots: 120g
  • Ginger: 1 small knob
  • Horn pepper: 1
  • Herbs: Vietnamese coriander, cilantro
  • Seasonings: seasoning powder, salt, rock sugar, MSG, fish sauce, ground pepper.

Steps

  1. Preparing and boiling the chicken
    • After purchasing the chicken, set the giblets aside and rub the inside and outside of the bird thoroughly with salt and lemon. Then, rinse it with water and let it drain.
    • Start by boiling the chicken. Pour 4 liters of water into a pot and heat until it begins to warm up. Then, add the chicken along with the shallots, a few thin slices of ginger, 1 tablespoon of salt, and 20g of rock sugar.
    • Bring the water to a boil over medium heat, skim off any foam, reduce the heat to low, and simmer the chicken for 15–20 minutes.
    • Once the chicken is cooked, turn off the heat and let it steep in the pot for another 15 minutes to prevent the bones from remaining red.
    • After 15 minutes, remove the chicken and place it in a bowl of ice water to firm up the meat.
  2. Prepare the remaining ingredients.
    • Peel the onion and slice it thinly. Soak the onion slices in a bowl of ice water for 15 minutes. Then, remove them and let them drain.
    • Peel, crush, and finely chop the garlic.
    • Peel the shallots; finely mince half of them and thinly slice the other half, then set them aside.
    • Remove the seeds from the horn pepper and cut it into thin strips.
    • Remove the stems from the hot peppers and finely chop them.
    • Wash the scallions thoroughly and separate the white parts from the green parts. Finely chop the green parts.
    • Wash the Vietnamese coriander thoroughly and chop it finely.
    • Cut the lime in half and squeeze out the juice.
    • Wash the mushrooms thoroughly and lightly score the caps for the congee.
  3. Preparing to cook chicken congee
    • Place a pan on the stove and sauté the chicken fat until fragrant. Add the rice and stir well over low heat. Once the grains have firmed up and turned opaque, turn off the heat.
    • For the chicken broth, remove the shallots and ginger.
    • Add more water to bring the total volume to 4 liters.
    • Add the rice and simmer over medium heat.
    • Next, place the pan back on the stove and sauté the mushrooms until fragrant. Season with a teaspoon of seasoning powder, sauté for another 2 minutes, then turn off the heat.
  4. Prepare the salad dressing
    • Place a pot on the stove and add 60g of sugar and 30g of fish sauce. Heat over low heat so the sugar dissolves quickly.
    • Once the sugar has dissolved, add 30g of lime juice and stir well.
    • Add the minced garlic and chili, stir well, and it's done.
  5. Make shredded chicken salad
    • First, shred the chicken for the salad. Chop off the thighs and set them aside. Shred the rest of the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Return the chicken bones to the pot of simmering porridge.
    • Next, place the onion in a large bowl and add 2.5 tablespoons of the salad dressing. Mix well and let it sit for 5 minutes to absorb the flavors.
    • Next, add the chicken and 3 tablespoons of the salad dressing. Mix well.
    • Add the Vietnamese coriander and shredded chili, then mix well again to finish.
  6. Complete the chicken congee
    • For the chicken congee, once the rice grains have expanded, remove the chicken bones and add the sautéed mushrooms.
    • Season with 1 tablespoon of seasoning powder, 1/3 tablespoon of salt, 1/3 tablespoon of MSG, and 1 tablespoon of sugar.
    • Add the scallion whites and ground pepper, then turn off the heat.
  7. Finished product and serving
    Transfer the shredded chicken and onion salad to a plate and sprinkle with fried shallots. Ladle the porridge into a bowl and also top it with fried shallots. Enjoy the salad with the porridge; you can serve it with the leftover salad dressing or a side of vegetarian salt-and-pepper dip. This chicken salad features a pleasant, mild tanginess, combining tender, sweet chicken with crisp, fresh onions — elements that blend harmoniously to create a uniquely refreshing flavor.
