Pho

Vietnamese national dish

Ingredients

  • Beef Soup Bone: Marrow bone
  • Herb for Broth: Dried cinnamon, black cardamom, dried tangerine peel, star anise, cloves, coriander seeds.
  • Rice Noodles
  • Beef: Beef shank, thin sliced beef.
  • Onion
  • Spices and Seasonings :Salt, fish sauce, seasoning powder, ginger, shallot, lemongrass.
  • Garnishes: scallions, culantro, cilantro, Thai basil, rice paddy herb
