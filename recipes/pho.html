Pho

Vietnamese national dish

Ingredients

  • Beef Soup Bone: Marrow bone
  • Herb for Broth: Dried cinnamon, black cardamom, dried tangerine peel, star anise, cloves, coriander seeds.
  • Rice Noodles
  • Beef: Beef shank, thin sliced beef.
  • Onion
  • Spices and Seasonings :Salt, fish sauce, seasoning powder, ginger, shallot, lemongrass.
  • Garnishes: scallions, culantro, cilantro, Thai basil, rice paddy herb

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients
    • Peel the onion, thinly slice one portion and cut the other into wedges.
    • Wash the cilantro thoroughly and chop it finely.
    • Separate the scallion white parts; finely chop the green parts.
    • Wash the accompanying vegetables and let them drain.
  2. Packet Pho seasoning Place the dried cinnamon, black cardamom, tangerine peel, star anise, clove, and coriander seeds into a clean cloth bag and tie it securely.
  3. Make beef pho broth:
    • Wash the beef marrow bones thoroughly. Place a pot of water on the stove and bring it to a boil. Briefly blanch the bones to remove any unpleasant odor.
    • Remove the blanched bones and place them on a tray along with ginger, shallots, and onion wedges; roast until the bones turn golden, then remove them from the oven and immediately transfer them into a bowl of ice water.
    • Bring a pot of water to a boil, then add the roasted beef bones, the prepared spice packet, onion, roasted ginger, and lemongrass. Simmer the bones for 5–6 hours to extract the flavor. While the bones are simmering, boil the beef shank until cooked through; be sure to remove the meat immediately once done, as leaving it in too long will cause it to become mushy. Finally, remove the solids and strain the liquid to obtain the clear broth.
    • Place the strained broth on the stove, season to taste with a little salt, MSG, seasoning powder, and rock sugar, bring to a boil, and turn off the heat.
  4. Plating and Enjoying
    • Wash the beef fillet and slice the lean meat.
    • Thinly sliced ​​beef shank.
    • Blanch the bean sprouts and scallion whites in boiling water.
    • Blanch the rice noodles in boiling water.
    • Place bean sprouts, scallion whites, and rice noodles into a bowl; arrange the beef shank, beef fillet, scallions, cilantro, onion, and sliced ​​chili on top, then pour in the broth.
    • Enjoy Pho with accompanying herbs and vegetables, chili sauce, and hoisin sauce.
